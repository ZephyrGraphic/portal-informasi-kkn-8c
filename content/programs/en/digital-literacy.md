## Introduction
In this digital age, access to technology and the ability to use it are key to unlocking broader educational opportunities. Our Digital Literacy program in Pangkalan Village aims to bridge the digital divide in local schools.

## Implementation
We conducted a series of workshops at SDN 01 Pangkalan covering:
- Introduction to computer hardware.
- Using cloud-based collaboration tools (Google Workspace).
- Internet security and digital ethics.

## Achievements
So far, we have trained 12 teachers and more than 100 students. The school now has a simple learning management system to help distribute lesson materials.

## Conclusion
Digital literacy is not just about learning to use a computer, but about empowering the youth of Pangkalan Village to compete in a global world.
