## Overview
Health is the foundation of community productivity. In Pangkalan Village, access to routine health facilities can sometimes be a challenge for residents living far from the district center.

## Our Activities
In collaboration with the Cikidang Health Center, we organized:
- Blood pressure and blood sugar checks.
- Nutritional consultation for pregnant women and toddlers (Stunting Prevention).
- Distribution of basic vitamins and supplements.

## Impact
More than 150 residents participated in this activity. We identified several health cases requiring immediate follow-up and helped refer them to more complete health facilities.
